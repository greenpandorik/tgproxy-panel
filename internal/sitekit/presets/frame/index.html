Portraits, places, honest light.

Independent photography studio for editorial and commercial work.

Portrait

Sittings run about ninety minutes, usually in daylight, usually with far fewer people in the room than clients expect. We photograph founders, writers, cooks and crews, and we send a first edit within four days.

Editorial

Commissions for magazines, weekend supplements and long-form features, shot on location with a small kit. We are used to short notice, tight corridors and one honest hour with the subject.

Places

Buildings, coastlines, interiors and the light that happens to be in them. This work is often made for architects and hotels, though a good deal of it began as personal travel and was licensed later.
