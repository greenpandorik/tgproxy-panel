Fast capture

A note opens the moment you press the key, wherever you are in the app. There is no template to pick and no folder to choose first, so a half-formed thought costs you nothing to write down.

Private spaces

Keep work, study and home in separate spaces that never mix in a list. Each space has its own tags and its own recent notes, and you can hide one entirely until you want it back.

Simple search

Type a few words and Luma looks through titles, body text and tags at once. Results keep the sentence around the match, so you can tell the right note from the near miss without opening either.
