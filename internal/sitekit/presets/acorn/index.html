Three things a team looks at every day

Acorn keeps the plan, the week's news and the numbers in one place, so nobody has to ask where the work stands.

  • Projects

    Each project holds its plan, its files and the people doing the work. Anyone on the team can open it and read the last three weeks without asking permission first.

  • Updates

    Every Friday Acorn asks each project for two lines and posts them together on Monday morning. Nobody writes a status report twice, and the archive keeps four years of them.

  • Insights

    Cycle time, carry-over and where the week actually went, drawn from work that was already logged. There is no separate timesheet to fill in on a Friday afternoon.
