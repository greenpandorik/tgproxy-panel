Global edge

Requests land in the nearest of seventy-two locations and stay on our backbone until they have to leave it. Anycast routing moves traffic away from a sick site within seconds, and you never change a DNS record to make that happen.

Smart caching

Orbit reads your cache headers and fills the gaps with rules you write in plain conditions. Stale responses keep serving while a single request goes back to origin, so a slow backend never turns into a slow page.
