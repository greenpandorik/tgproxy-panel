We design the small parts that hold everything together.

Ferrule Studio is an independent practice working in identity, signage and print for workshops, foundries and the people who run them. Founded 2016, based in a converted bindery.

Selected work

Alder & Vane

Identity and signage for a tool-making cooperative — a mark built from a single punch stroke, applied across stamped metal and letterpress stock.

Kettleworks Almanac

A yearly almanac for a foundry collective: type-driven covers, one colour per edition, printed on uncoated stock that ages with the workshop.

Common Bench

Wayfinding and bench numbering for a shared workshop space, designed to survive sawdust, oil and repeated repainting.

How we work

  1. Listen first — a half day on site, in the workshop, before any brief is written.
  2. Draw by hand — every mark starts on paper before it touches a screen.
  3. Prove it in the material — proofs on the actual stock, metal or surface.
  4. Leave you the files — no dependency on us to reprint or resize later.
