Our space

Eighteen seats, four tables and a bench along the window. No music before nine. The back wall is plaster and the counter is a single piece of ash that our neighbour Toshi planed for us over a long weekend in 2021.

People work here, read here and meet here. We ask only that calls are taken outside, and we will happily bring your cup out to you.
