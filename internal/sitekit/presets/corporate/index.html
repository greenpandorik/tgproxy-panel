Practical solutions for growing organizations.

Independent advisory and technology services for modern businesses.

We are a partner-led firm of seventy-four people. We are engaged when a decision matters more than a report: a plant that has outgrown its planning, a finance function that cannot close in under three weeks, a board that needs a defensible answer before it commits capital.

We take a small number of engagements at a time and staff them with the people who sold the work. No panels, no offshore delivery centre, and no recommendation we would not implement ourselves.
